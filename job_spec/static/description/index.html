Zakheni ICT Recruitment Module


Zakheni Recruitment transforms Odoo default recruitment app into a complete, automated hiring solution that integrates directly with HR, Timesheets, and Payroll.
It is built for real HR teams that want less manual admin, better visibility, and faster onboarding without juggling spreadsheets or email threads.
Designed and developed by Zakheni ICT (Pty) Ltd, a leading South African Odoo solutions provider, this module gives you an end to end workflow.
